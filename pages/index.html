THE PROBLEM

If you put AI on top of a mess, you get a faster mess.

Copilot can reach everything a person can reach in SharePoint, Teams and OneDrive. Most organisations have years of copies, deep folders and old sharing links sitting there. Copilot finds all of it.

01

Overshared

A board paper in a folder shared with "everyone" two years ago is now one prompt away from every staff member.

02

Out of date

Final_final_v7 is not a filing strategy. With five versions of a policy, Copilot picks one, and it may not be the right one.

03

Hard to find

Folders ten levels deep and no consistent labels mean people can't find things, and neither can Copilot.

WHAT I REVIEW

The three foundations Copilot stands on.

SharePoint

Sites, hubs, libraries, folders and metadata. Is there one clear home for every team's information, and can it be found?

Teams

Teams with no owners, channels nobody uses, guests who never left. Tidy collaboration, clear naming and rules for when to use what.

OneDrive

Business files hiding in personal storage and shared with open links. Where they belong and how to move them safely.
