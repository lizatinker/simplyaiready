QUESTIONS ABOUT PRICING

Where should we start?

With the $890 Quick Check. You'll know where you stand, exactly what needs to change and how to get there. Many organisations take the roadmap and get on with it themselves.

What's the difference between the Quick Check and the Discovery?

The Quick Check works from your Microsoft 365 reports and tells you what needs to change. The Discovery involves your people: I run workshops with your teams and design the information architecture for your whole SharePoint environment.

How is the Discovery priced?

It depends on the size of your organisation and the number of workshops needed. The typical investment starts at $2,500 to $3,500, and you get a fixed price after a free 15-minute chat, before any work starts.

Is the Quick Check fee wasted if we go on to a Discovery?

No. The full $890 comes off your Discovery if you go ahead within 60 days.

Do we have to use you to build it?

No. If your IT team or another partner will build it, add the Build-ready Blueprint and they'll have every step written out. If we build it, you don't need the Blueprint.

Can we just buy some hours?

Yes. Hours come in blocks of 5 for $1,250, to use within 6 months on whatever you need. For regular help, monthly support is $1,450 a month with a discount for a 12-month sign-up.

How do payments work?

The Quick Check is paid upfront. For a Discovery, 50% starts the work and 50% is due at your presentation. A build is paid 50% at the start of each stage.

Are prices in Australian dollars?

Yes, all prices are AUD and exclude GST. Workshops can be online or face to face.
